# Aditus Belli — Game Idea

A living scratchpad of concepts and scenarios. Nothing here is final: these are
ideas and features that may or may not make it into the game.

## Modes

Two modes: **Story** and **Skirmish** (random match).

### Story

A global map divided into several sectors. As you clear sectors you advance
through historical periods, and the modes of warfare change with them — which
also keeps the difficulty rising as you progress.

The goal of each sector is not necessarily to unlock something brand new: it is
to place the player in a different context that teaches a particular concept of
warfare. Some sectors may simply reinforce or recombine earlier ideas.

| Period | Focus / what the player learns |
|---|---|
| Egypt | Basics of construction and resources |
| Greece | Basics of combat (the Spartan phalanx) |
| Rome | Conquering Italy with slightly more complex combat; basics of economy |
| Punic Wars | Basics of supply and strategy; naval combat |
| Crusades | Siege weapons |
| American War of Independence | Firearms and cannons |
| American territorial expansion | More efficient transport (railways) |
| Napoleonic Wars | Diplomacy, military coordination, exploiting terrain without supply lines |
| WW1 | Trenches, bombardment, tanks |
| WW2 | Espionage |
| Vietnam | Guerrilla warfare |
| Yugoslav Wars | Urban combat |

### Skirmish (random match)

As in Age of Empires: choose the match settings, spawn on a randomly generated
map, and fight against the AI.
